What stands behind every visit.

The people, equipment and checks that let us promise the same result in a family apartment, an expat villa or a floor of company residences.

How we are equipped

People

Every professional is our employee, verified before joining and qualified in one trade: licensed electricians, trained plumbers and carpenters, brand-trained appliance technicians.

Equipment

Insulation and earth testers, leak and moisture meters, drain machines, gas gauges and a full carpentry kit, calibrated on a schedule and carried to every visit.

Parts

Genuine parts from authorised suppliers, shown to you before fitting and billed at the printed price. The replaced part is handed back.

Quality control

The same four checks on every visit, whoever the customer is.

Before
A confirmation call records the fault, the brand and model, and access, so the right person and parts arrive.
During
Diagnosis first, then a fixed quote. Each job follows a written safety and testing checklist.
At the end
The work is tested in front of you, the area is cleaned, and you sign off before we leave.
After
A follow-up call the next day, and a free return within 30 days if the same fault comes back.
