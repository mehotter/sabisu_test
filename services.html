Before we arrive

Four small things that help the team give you their full time.

  1. Put away jewellery, cash and documents you would rather keep private.
  2. Tell us about pets, infants or anyone resting at home.
  3. Point out anything fragile, antique or recently repaired.
  4. Keep one tap and one power socket free for the team's equipment.

Questions before you book

Do I need to provide equipment or supplies?

No. Each team brings its own machines, cloths and cleaning products. If you prefer particular products, or someone at home has allergies, tell us on the confirmation call and we will use yours.

Is the price really fixed?

Yes. The price depends on the service and the size of your home, and you see it before you confirm. It does not change on the day, even if the work takes longer than we planned.

Will the same people come each time?

For housekeeping and any repeat booking, yes. If your regular professional is on leave, we send a stand-in you have been introduced to beforehand.

What if I need to change the day?

You can move or cancel a visit without charge up to 12 hours before it starts. Call us or reply to your confirmation message.

Do I need to be at home?

Someone should be there at the start and for the lead's walk-through at the end. In between, you are free to get on with your day.

Which areas do you serve?

Select neighbourhoods in Bengaluru for now. Share your address when you reserve and we will tell you straight away whether we can reach you.

Not sure which service fits?

Tell us about your home and we will suggest one. Lines are open 8 am to 8 pm, every day.
