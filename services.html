Before we arrive

Four small things that help us finish in one visit.

  1. Tell us the brand and model of any appliance, if you can find it.
  2. Send a photo of the problem on WhatsApp when you reserve.
  3. Clear the area around the fault so we can reach it.
  4. Let building security know we are coming, with our names.

Questions before you book

Is the price really fixed?

Yes. After the diagnosis you get one price for labour and parts. Work starts only when you approve it, and nothing is added afterwards without your say.

What does the warranty cover?

Our workmanship, for 30 days. If the same fault returns, we come back free. Parts carry their manufacturer's warranty, and we give you the bill.

Can you fit parts I have already bought?

Yes. We charge labour only and warrant our fitting, though not the part itself.

Which appliance brands do you service?

Most major brands sold in India. Tell us the brand and model when you reserve and we will confirm before the visit.

What if I need to change the day?

Move or cancel a visit without charge up to 12 hours before it starts.

Which areas do you serve?

Select neighbourhoods in Bengaluru for now. Share your address when you reserve and we will tell you straight away whether we can reach you.

Not sure which service you need?

Describe the problem and we will send the right person. Lines are open 8 am to 8 pm, every day.
