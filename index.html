What the name means

サービス

sa · bi · su

Sabisu is how the word “service” is written and spoken in Japanese. We borrowed it because Japanese hospitality carries an idea we admire: notice what a guest will need, and attend to it before they have to ask.

That is the standard we train for. A good repair is one you never have to think about again.

Three things we believe

A short list is a promise

Every service we add divides our attention. Four is the number we can staff with real specialists today, so four is where we start.

The person matters more than the product

Tools and parts are easy to buy. Care is not. We hire slowly, pay fairly and keep our teams together, because the same hands do better work each time they return.

Leave nothing to be asked for

Dust swept up. The old part handed back. The switch tested twice. Small things nobody requests are how you can tell someone was paying attention.

The star

Our mark is a single star. It stands for the standard we aim at on every visit, and for the quiet prestige of a home that is always ready.

Olive green is our colour because it is calm, natural and steady. Golden sand is kept for the star and a few highlights.

Is there a charge just for the visit?

There is a small inspection fee, shown when you reserve. If you go ahead with the repair, it is taken off the bill.

Is the price really fixed?

Yes. After the diagnosis the technician gives you one price for labour and parts. Work starts only when you approve it.

Do you bring the parts?

Common parts travel with the technician. Anything unusual is ordered and fitted on a second visit at no extra visit charge.

How do I pay?

After the work is tested. We accept UPI, cards and bank transfer, and send a receipt the same day.

Do you handle emergencies?

Call us between 8 am and 8 pm. For a burst pipe or a power failure we send the nearest free technician first.
