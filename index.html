How a visit goes

  1. You reserve

    Choose a service and a day. The fixed price is shown before you confirm.

  2. We call

    Within two working hours we confirm the time and ask about your home: surfaces, pets, anything fragile.

  3. The team arrives

    In uniform and on time, carrying their own equipment and supplies.

  4. The lead inspects

    Before leaving, the lead walks each room with you and puts right anything that falls short.

  5. We check in

    One call the next day. If something was missed, we return within 48 hours at no charge.

Do I need to provide equipment or supplies?

No. Each team brings its own machines, cloths and products. If you prefer particular products, or someone at home has allergies, tell us on the confirmation call.

Is the price really fixed?

Yes. It depends on the service and the size of your home, and you see it before you confirm. It does not change on the day.

How do I pay?

After the visit, once the lead's walk-through is done. We accept UPI, cards and bank transfer, and send a receipt the same day.

Are the products safe for children and pets?

We use low-odour, non-caustic products by default and keep stronger descalers for bathrooms only. Tell us about pets or infants and the team will plan around them.

What if I need to change the day?

Move or cancel a visit without charge up to 12 hours before it starts.
